## Brand & Style

This design system serves a dual-sided marketplace platform combining high-intent service procurement with an authentic, community-driven social feed. It bridges transactional confidence with organic discovery, allowing clients and certified service providers to interact, inspect previous deliverables, and transact safely.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Core
- **Authoritative Credibility:** Rooted in institutional clarity, structured safeguards, and certified competence. Users must feel absolute financial and operational safety.
- **Dynamic Energy:** Propelled by warm, actionable accents signaling real-world craftsmanship, momentum, and immediate response times.
- **Radical Transparency:** Clear social proofs, unfiltered before-and-after project comparisons, explicit pricing structures, and unembellished peer reviews.

### Design Movement
**Modern SaaS & High-Trust Marketplace:** A hybrid execution combining functional utility with lifestyle editorial warmth. It balances dense data structures (quotes, scopes of work, transaction logs) with rich visual portfolios. Depth is established through crisp surface division, gentle tonal layering, and purposeful micro-elevations rather than heavy skeuomorphism or loud decorative flourishes.

## Colors

The palette directly operationalizes the platform's dual mission of verified trust and dynamic interaction:

### Palette Structure
- **Primary Navy (`#174A70`):** Anchors global navigation, critical CTA states, verified corporate framing, and primary typographic weight. Signifies institutional security, escrow stability, and compliance.
- **Secondary Amber Orange (`#E67E35`):** Reserved for conversion triggers, "Book Now" prompts, active bids, live project milestones, and notification dots. Conveys human warmth and swift action.
- **Tertiary Teal/Cyan (`#0D9488`):** Dedicated exclusively to verification architecture—certified badges, background tint fills on verified account headers, identity confirmations, and success telemetry.
- **Canvas Neutral (`#F3F5F7`):** A cool, low-saturation backdrop that provides eye comfort during deep browsing sessions, deliberately separating pure white interactive cards from the background.
- **Surface White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast containment for marketplace cards, social feed posts, conversation threads, and input fields.
- **Typography Tokens:**
  - `text-slate-900` (`#1E293B`): Deep slate for primary headlines, card titles, and high-readability body copy.
  - `text-slate-500` (`#64748B`): Muted slate for secondary meta-data, time stamps, ratings counts, and captioning.
  - `border-subtle` (`#E2E8F0`): Hairline definition for inputs, dividers, and card borders.

## Typography

The platform utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve a clean, modern, and accessible interface. Its geometric construction guarantees maximum legibility on service sheets and spec grids, while its friendly curved junctions bring approachable humanity to personal portfolio feeds and social feedback loops.

### Rules of Usage
- **Numerics & Pricing:** Use `label-lg` or `headline-md` with `fontWeight: 700` and tabular numeric layout (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to align currency and rating values.
- **Editorial Post Headers:** Use `headline-sm` with tight negative tracking (`-0.01em`) to maintain an authoritative, editorial cadence without feeling aggressive.
- **Metadata and Tags:** Never use standard body weights below 13px; micro-content must use `label-sm` with bold weight (`700`) and expanded letter spacing for effortless scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt architectural rhythm, with a max canvas constraint of `1280px` on desktop viewport sizes to ensure readability of multi-column service matrices.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Composition
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stack. Outer margin is fixed to `1rem` (`margin-sm`). Gutter between feed elements is `1rem`. Feed cards span 100% viewport width minus margins, and action bars dock permanently to the bottom thumb zone with integrated blur backdrops.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column adaptive grid. Outer margin expands to `1.5rem`. Service matrices display in 2-column arrangements, while the social feed retains an offset 6-column main stream with a 2-column persistent filter rail.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column balanced grid with `1.5rem` gutters (`gutter`). Typical page split comprises a 3-column sticky provider navigation/meta rail, a 6-column dynamic activity/listing feed, and a 3-column dynamic conversion/proposal sidebar.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a layered surface model with restrained ambient shadows tinted by the primary navy tone, eliminating harsh gray drops.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F3F5F7` flat ground plane.
- **Level 1 (Feed & Content Cards):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(23, 74, 112, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(23, 74, 112, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Interactive Cards, Dropdowns):** `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(23, 74, 112, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(23, 74, 112, 0.04)`. Card elements slightly ascend (-2px Y translation) on hover to communicate affordance.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Proposal Drawers, Floating Chat Dock):** `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(23, 74, 112, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(23, 74, 112, 0.06)`. Border switches to pure `#CBD5E1` for crisper foreground detachment.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on generous curvature (`rounded-xl` / Level 2), imparting a soft, approachable feel suited for contemporary social workflows while preserving professional crispness.

### Application Specs
- **Cards, Modals, and Media Containers:** Standardize on `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for outer borders. Nested media (such as project photos) takes a proportional `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) to avoid corner clipping distortion.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** Inputs and standard buttons use `rounded-lg` (0.75rem to 1rem). 
- **Tags, Filter Pills, and Verification Badges:** Full pill execution (`rounded-full`) to immediately delineate interactive attributes and status indicators from structural rectangular cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Direct Contact / Hire / Submit):** Deep Navy (`#174A70`) background with solid white text. Padding: `12px 24px`. Rounded: `12px`. Hover: darken to `#123957` with ambient shadow.
- **High-Conversion Action (Immediate Book / Special Offer):** Amber Orange (`#E67E35`) background, white text. Focused solely on high-value transactions.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, Navy stroke (`1.5px solid #174A70`), text `#174A70`. Hover fills to `rgba(23, 74, 112, 0.05)`.

### Verified Badges & Trust Accents
- **Provider Verification Chip:** Pill container with an emerald-cyan tint background (`#CCFBF1`), emerald border (`#5EEAD4`), deep teal copy (`#0F766E`), featuring a solid teal checkmark icon. Placed directly next to provider handle/name across all surfaces.

### Cards: Social Showcase & Listings
- **Social Project Card:** Pure white background, `24px` internal padding, `rounded-xl`, subtle navy outline. Includes:
  - Header: Provider avatar, verified badge, location, and timestamp.
  - Body: Interactive before/after split viewer with a draggable divider or clean juxtaposed dual-aspect frames with corner tags ("Before" / "After").
  - Footer: Project budget estimate pill, category tags, client endorsement quotation, and direct message CTA.
- **Service Offering Card:** Fixed grid item with a 16:9 featured imagery slot, price badge pinned to the top right, rating cluster (`#E67E35` star + review count), and an immediate "Request Quote" secondary trigger.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`. Focus state: Border transitions to `#174A70` with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(23, 74, 112, 0.15)` focus ring. Placeholder text styled in `#94A3B8`.
- Inline validation: Positive states marked with `#0D9488` icons; error messages in clear crimson (`#EF4444`).

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Height: `32px`. Text style: `label-sm`.
- Default: `#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` text.
- Active Filter: `#174A70` background, `#FFFFFF` text.

### Interactive Before/After Comparisons
- Contained in an overflow-hidden `rounded-lg` box. Provides visual anchors with high-contrast semi-transparent pills: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)` backdrop with white text for clear state orientation.
